The tape

Sculpted ugly, on purpose

Claysons are 1,300 horror characters made to look hand-pressed from matte plasticine, lit like a stop-motion set and shot against thumb-printed clay backdrops.

Every one is disfigured differently: swollen skulls, droopy eyes, busted teeth, stitched skin. Each holds a weapon in a clenched fist, turned three-quarters toward you like they just heard something. No two share the same face, gear and backdrop.

The collection runs from back-alley Commons up to 13 Legendary 1/1s, each an original character with traits no other Clayson has.
